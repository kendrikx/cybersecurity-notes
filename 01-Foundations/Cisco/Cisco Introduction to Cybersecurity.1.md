# Cisco Introduction to Cybersecurity 1

## What is Cybersecurity

Cybersecurity is the practice of protecting:
- systems
- networks
- devices
- applications
- data

from:
- cyberattacks
- unauthorized access
- data theft
- digital damage

---

# Levels of Cybersecurity

## 1. Personal Security

Focuses on protecting:
- personal devices
- online accounts
- passwords
- personal information

Examples:
- strong passwords
- multi-factor authentication
- avoiding phishing attacks

---

## 2. Organizational Security

Organizations protect:
- employee data
- customer data
- company infrastructure
- internal systems

Security measures include:
- firewalls
- access control
- monitoring systems
- cybersecurity policies

---

## 3. Government Security

Governments protect:
- national infrastructure
- military systems
- citizen information
- public services

Cyberattacks against governments can affect:
- transportation
- healthcare
- banking
- national security

---

## Medical Data
Examples:

## Financial Data
Examples:

- payment records

## Educational Data
Examples:

---

# Personal Data Protection

## Offline Protection
- secure physical documents
- lock devices
- avoid sharing sensitive information carelessly

## Online Protection
- use strong passwords
- enable MFA
- avoid suspicious links
- update software regularly

---

# Identity Theft

Identity theft occurs when someone steals personal information to:
- impersonate another person
- access financial accounts
- commit fraud

Examples of stolen information:
- passwords
- ID numbers
- bank details
- email credentials

---

# Organizations and Groups Interested in Personal Data

## Internet Service Providers (ISPs)
Can monitor:
- browsing activity
- internet usage

## Advertisers
Collect data to:
- target advertisements
- analyze behavior

## Social Media Platforms
Collect:
- personal preferences
- interactions
- uploaded content

## Cybercriminals
Steal data for:
- fraud
- phishing
- blackmail
- unauthorized access
